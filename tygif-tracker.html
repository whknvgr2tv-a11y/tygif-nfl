Money Rule

Always bet against the team holding most of the money: that's where the sportsbooks are exposed.

Fade the Crowd
Most of the money and most of the bets are on one team. We bet the other team.
Heavy money (65%+)
Same, when that team has 65% or more of the money.
Big Favorite Fade
The favorite is giving 6+ points and has the money. We take the underdog.
Sharps
The money is on one team but most bets are on the other (big bettors vs the public). We pass and just track it.

How the model works

The rules

The money rule
We always look at which team has most of the money bet on it. That is the sportsbooks' risk, so we bet the other side. How many bets are on each side only tells us what kind of game it is.
Fade the Crowd
Money and bets on the same team: the public is all over it. Bet the other team. This is the main play.
Big Favorite Fade
A 6+ point favorite that also has the money: take the underdog with the points.
Sharps (we pass)
Money on one team, most bets on the other. Usually big bettors. Betting against them lost money early in the season, so these are tracked, not bet.
Sportsbooks disagree (we pass)
DraftKings' money is on one team but the money across all sportsbooks is on the other. Tracked, not bet.
Totals
Overs and unders are tracked only. Betting against the money on totals has lost so far.
Ratings
STRONG = Fade the Crowd and money 65%+ (the hammer — 12-0-3 so far). LEAN = smaller edge or a ding on the card. PASS = no bet. A rating drops a level when the line moves against us, signals are mixed, or our QB is out/doubtful/questionable. Each play card shows a plain checklist so you can see why it is a hammer vs a lean.

Bet against the money

Standing rule: always against the side holding most of the money (books' exposure). Bet % is context only. 'Fading the public' = money and bets on the same side; 'Fading the sharps' = money on one side, bets on the other. Big favorite angle = favorite 6+ AND money on the favorite -> play the dog ATS. Splits: DraftKings first when a pre-kickoff snapshot exists, otherwise all-books closing consensus.

Trends we're watching

A split is flagged the first time it shows 10+ decided games and cover % (or over %) ≥ 60% or ≤ 40%. Flag date/time and the record at flag are frozen on the day it was flagged and never reset. 'Since flagged' counts ONLY final games kicking off after the flag timestamp. Lean $ = $100/bet at -110 on the lean side. Exact mirror splits (e.g. Home underdogs = Road favorites) are tracked once, under the favorite split. Retired = the lean went 40% or worse over 8+ decided games since it was flagged (kept on the list, no longer used on cards).
