How tygif's model works

Tracker ›

Short version for the group. Flat $100/bet on the card. Lines move — this is a process, not gospel.

The money rule

Find the side with most of the money (DraftKings first, all books second). That is the books' exposure. We bet the other side.

Fade the Crowd = money and bets on the same team. That is the main play type.

What STRONG means

A hammer. Needs Fade the Crowd and money at 65%+. Weeks 1–4 that bar went 12-0-3 (+$1,095). Mid-money spots stay LEAN on purpose — optional for the group, not hammers.

STRONG · full-size feel LEAN · about half QB ding · cut further

When we pass

  • Sharps split — money one side, most bets the other. Fading those lost early; we track both ways, don't bet.
  • Sources disagree — DraftKings' money side ≠ all-books money side. Tracked, not played.
  • Thin money — under 55% with no big-fav angle. No edge.

What can ding a play

Mixed signals, line moved against us, or our QB out/doubtful/questionable — each drops the rating one level. Soft watches (divisional dog, primetime home dog, off-TNF, etc.) are badges only — they do not flip the play.

Cowboys note

Any Dallas game gets a heads-up. Stick to the money rule — the call comes from the numbers, not the logo.
