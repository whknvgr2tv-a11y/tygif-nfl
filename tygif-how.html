How it works

Tracker

Short share for the group — same rules as the tracker Money Rule tab. Flat $100/bet on the card. Card audit lives under Results. Lines move. Process, not gospel.

The money rule

Find the side with most of the money (DraftKings first, all books second). That is the books' exposure. We bet the other side.

Fade the Crowd = money and bets on the same team. That is the main play type.

What STRONG means

12-0-3 · +$1,095

A hammer. Needs Fade the Crowd and money at 65%+ (DraftKings split first; the all-books split must agree on the side). That is the Weeks 1–4 record at $100/bet. LEAN = a smaller edge (money under 65%) or a STRONG that took a ding. Mid-money spots stay LEAN on purpose — optional for the group, not hammers.

STRONG · top rating LEAN · optional QB ding · drops one level

When we pass

  • Sharps split — money one side, most bets the other. Fading those lost early; we track both ways, don't bet.
  • Sources disagree — DraftKings' money side ≠ all-books money side. Tracked, not played.
  • Thin money — under 55% with no big-fav angle. No edge.

What can ding a play

Mixed signals, line moved more than 1 pt toward the money, or our QB out/doubtful/questionable — each drops the rating one level. Soft watches (divisional dog, primetime home dog, off-TNF, etc.) are badges only — they do not flip the play.

Cowboys note

Any Dallas game gets a heads-up. Stick to the money rule — the call comes from the numbers, not the logo.
