How it works

The rule

  • Fade side: the team against most of the money. Our STRONG and LEAN spread calls are always the fade side.
  • Money side: the team holding most of the money (tracking only).
  • The money side is tracked for trends only. It is never a play.
  • Spreads and moneylines, favorite or underdog.
  • Money and bets: all books (ScoresAndOdds). DraftKings must have the money on the same team.

Game types

Checked in this order: 4, 5, 1, 2, 3.

  1. Fade the big favorite: 65%+ of the money on a favorite giving 6+ points. Take the underdog.
  2. Fade the crowd: 65%+ of the money and most bets on one team. Take the other team.
  3. Crowd leaning: 55–64% of the money on one team. LEAN at most; money and bets on different teams is a pass.
  4. Books disagree: all books and DraftKings have the money on different teams. PASS.
  5. No clear crowd: under 55% of the money on either team. PASS.

Ratings

  • STRONG: 65%+ of the money, most bets on the same team, DraftKings money on the same team (its bets need not agree).
  • LEAN: 55–64% of the money, or a STRONG that drops one level.
  • PASS: types 4 and 5, money and bets on different teams, or a LEAN that drops.
  • Drop one level if the line moves more than 1 point toward the money from the Monday line This never changes the type.
  • Monday line: average of TeamRankings books 2 and 3, each book’s last quote before Monday 12:00 AM ET (quotes older than 7 days skipped).

Moneyline

  • Same team as every STRONG or LEAN play, favorite or underdog, graded at the DraftKings closing moneyline.
  • Grouped by rating, type, and favorite or underdog. Posted only when that group has a profitable moneyline record to date.
  • A tie on the moneyline is a push, $0.

Records

  • Flat $100 per play, graded at the DraftKings closing line and price. A loss is −$100; a push is $0.
  • Every week to date. STRONG, LEAN, PASS, fade side, money side and moneyline are kept separate, never combined.
  • Weeks 1–4: all-books splits only; DraftKings split not recorded.
  • Rules v3 · Oct 15, 2026: both sides of every game tracked, grouped by rating, type and side (fade/money; moneyline also favorite/underdog). Plays are only our STRONG/LEAN spread calls and our moneyline on the same team, posted when its group is above $0 from earlier weeks. The money side and PASS-game sides are tracked for trends only and are never a play. If both teams qualify on the spread, only our original call posts.
